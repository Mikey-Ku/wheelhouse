How it works

Four steps, 17 picks
01 The wheel spins twice. First a team, then one of its players. You choose neither.
02 You take one stat off him. His arm, his legs, his hands. Each stat goes once per position, so spending it early costs you later.
03 You get his last six weeks. How often he cleared this number before, and against whom. You never get this week's result.
04 Every call in, then the truth. The whole week resolves at once and prints as a slip against what actually happened.
